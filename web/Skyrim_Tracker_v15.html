SPELLS
Spells
A spell is bound to the mind through study, not chance - read a Spell Tome, or be taught it directly, to register it as Obtained.
ENCHANTING EFFECTS
Enchanting Effects
Knowledge of an enchantment is earned through destruction, not discovery - disenchant an item bearing the effect at an Arcane Enchanter to register it as Learned.
BOOKS
Books
A library is proven by its shelf, not its reading - place the book in one of your tracked homes to register it as Obtained.
DRAGON SHOUTS
Dragon Shouts
A Word of Power only settles into memory once proven - learn it at a Word Wall, unlock it with a dragon soul, and shout it at least once to register it as Learned.
PERKS
Perks
Most perks demand a point spent once the skill is high enough, but a handful answer to deeds alone - the matching quest perk registers as Obtained simply by finishing its quest, no perk point required.
COLLECTIBLE ITEMS
Collectible Items
A collection is proven by its display, not its discovery - place the item in one of your tracked homes to register it as Obtained.
LOCATIONS
Locations
The map remembers only what you have seen - travel within sight of the location to register it as Discovered.
INGREDIENTS
Ingredients
Nature reveals her secrets one bite at a time - consume the ingredient raw or brew it into a potion to learn its properties, all 4 must be tasted before it registers as Discovered.
